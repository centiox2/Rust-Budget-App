# Visual assets

The supplied image remains the visual reference. The application code was rewritten in Kotlin. The generated gold artwork and bundled fonts from the previous Verdant design were retained.

- `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/coins.png`: generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this app. Prompt: “Create a premium 3D gold coin artwork asset for an Android finance app onboarding screen. Portrait 2:3 composition, pure black #000000 background. A very large thick polished gold dollar coin floating near upper center-right, turned 22 degrees to show its beveled ridged edge, embossed dollar sign, luxurious warm yellow gold material and realistic studio reflections, like high-end fintech marketing renders. One smaller matching gold dollar coin floating near lower left. On the right half, thin near-black vertical glass slats with subtle green and gold edge reflections partly occlude the large coin, as seen in modern black-and-green fintech onboarding references. Keep everything in upper 85% of canvas. No text, no interface, no phone, no logos, no gradients in background beyond subtle local gold glow. Output a standalone artwork image to be used inside the app.”
- `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/drawable/ribbon.png`: generated with the built-in image-generation tool for the polished Kotlin UI. Prompt: “Create one premium fintech app illustration asset on a fully transparent background: a sculptural folded ribbon, like a lush abstract fan of 6 broad curled dark-green leaves made of glossy folded satin paper. The folded structure curls from the lower right corner, sweeping upward and left, with the tallest broad rounded fold at upper left and progressively shorter curling folds to the right. Luxurious 3D studio render, rich near-black forest green in shadows, dark emerald surfaces, bright lime rim highlights along thin edges, soft self shadows, elegant smooth rounded curves. Front three-quarter view. Wide 3:2 composition. Keep the whole sculpture within the image with a small transparent margin. No text, no logo, no money, no coins, no background, no flat vector shapes, no plant veins. This will sit on the right side of a black rounded finance app banner. The result should resemble premium green folded-paper abstract 3D artwork rather than a literal plant.”
- `shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/font/`: bundled Poppins from the Google Fonts repository, distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.
- App icon: original leaf artwork implemented as an Android vector drawable.

The welcome screen, centered balance, four actions, and analytics category cards follow the reference composition. Home was subsequently simplified at the user's request: its balance header retains the liquid backdrop, followed by a solid background with uncontained transaction rows, without the ribbon banner. The unused ribbon asset remains available in the source artwork. Budget-specific controls use income, expense, and category budgets rather than suggesting actual banking services.
