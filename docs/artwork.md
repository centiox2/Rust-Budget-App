# Visual assets

The supplied image is a visual reference; no prior app source or assets were reused.

- `public/coins.png`: generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this app. Prompt: “Create a premium 3D gold coin artwork asset for an Android finance app onboarding screen. Portrait 2:3 composition, pure black #000000 background. A very large thick polished gold dollar coin floating near upper center-right, turned 22 degrees to show its beveled ridged edge, embossed dollar sign, luxurious warm yellow gold material and realistic studio reflections, like high-end fintech marketing renders. One smaller matching gold dollar coin floating near lower left. On the right half, thin near-black vertical glass slats with subtle green and gold edge reflections partly occlude the large coin, as seen in modern black-and-green fintech onboarding references. Keep everything in upper 85% of canvas. No text, no interface, no phone, no logos, no gradients in background beyond subtle local gold glow. Output a standalone artwork image to be used inside the app.”
- `public/ribbon.svg`: original vector artwork implemented in SVG.
- `public/fonts/`: bundled Poppins from the Google Fonts repository, distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.
- App icon: original leaf artwork generated in code, exported to Tauri icon sizes.

The welcome screen, centered balance, green lighting, four actions, ribbon banner, transaction cards, and analytics category cards follow the reference composition. Budget-specific controls use income, expense, and category budgets rather than suggesting actual banking services.
